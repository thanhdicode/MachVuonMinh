## Current creative-web lessons
1. One governing interaction beats a pile of components.
2. Sequence beats stacked slides.
3. Spatial metaphor makes abstract systems understandable.
4. Effects should transition meaning, not decorate.
5. DOM and WebGL must feel like one composition.
6. Premium work is often restrained in UI but exact in motion.
