# Art direction

**Creative thesis:** revolutionary print becomes a living machine. The red thread changes from furrow to belt, signal, data stream, lab pulse, Vietnam path and adaptive loop. Structures around it express production relations. Follow `00_NORTH_STAR.md`, `02_DESIGN_SYSTEM_V2.md` and `03_SCENE_BY_SCENE_V2.md` for the approved concept and scene sequence.

Before building a scene, compare 2–3 directions on a single visual idea, one signature motion and one bespoke detail. Choose the direction where the red thread and the scene's meaning are clearest. Use `.studio/frontendmaxxing/art-direction.skill.md`, `3d-web.skill.md` and `taste.skill.md` as references, then inspect the project specs and select only relevant techniques.

The approved palette is paper `#F3E8D0`, ink `#171512`, red `#B51F2A`, with brass, oxide and steel accents. Use Be Vietnam Pro for title/body and IBM Plex Mono for labels. Avoid card grids, neon SaaS styling, weak flat illustrations and repeated centered compositions. Each screenshot needs one dominant object and a visible, meaningful red thread.

The implemented directions and rejected alternatives are recorded in `SCENE-DIRECTIONS.md`. Visual verification lives in `qa/rebuild/`; the first torus and approximate map were rejected and replaced by a machined collar and a sourced geographic atlas.
