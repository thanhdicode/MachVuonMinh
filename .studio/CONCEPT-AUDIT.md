# Concept audit — 28 September 2026

Topic: Lực lượng sản xuất mới trong kỷ nguyên vươn mình của dân tộc và yêu cầu thay đổi quan hệ sản xuất ở Việt Nam hiện nay.

The current journey explains technological change but under-explains people and economic relationships. Its Vietnam chapter mainly quotes strategy documents. A visitor can remember a red ring without understanding ownership, production organization or distribution. Historical machinery must serve the contemporary argument rather than dominate it.

Keep the red thread as productive capacity; make its surrounding structure legible. Introduce the metaphor at entry with two short labels. In the digital scene, let recognizable data, computing and skill objects replace anonymous node markers. In the Lab, describe the outer structure in three concrete questions: who owns/accesses productive means, who organizes production, how products/benefits are distributed. Do not equate QHSX with institutions, or equate higher slider values with a better society.

## Three possible Vietnam compositions

1. Documentary photo wall: authentic, but rights and competing images obstruct the map.
2. One large statistic: memorable, but weak on causal explanation.
3. Evidence atlas with one tangible vignette at a time: keep geography and offshore labels; show a worker/tool/system, one bounded fact, and a three-part relationship bridge.

Choose 3. Use original illustrations explicitly labeled as illustrations, not photos of the cited site. Keep exact dates, attribution and limitations in the source drawer.

## Implementation slices

1. Verify sources: HTX Thâm Triều's use of contracted drone services; VinFast's documented robot/MES production; NSO's 2024 digital-economy value-added share. Keep historical dates visible, never imply an old count is a current inventory.
2. Add three evidence cases with a compact fact, object, map locator and ownership/organization/distribution questions. Separate factual observations from proposed responses.
3. Connect entry, digital, Lab and ending copy to the same causal argument. Replace abstract markers with recognizable production objects. Preserve keyboard, wheel and reduced-motion behavior.
4. Capture all changed scenes at 1440×900 and 390×844, inspect object/copy overlaps, exercise case navigation and source links, run existing interaction checks and production build.

Acceptance: the visitor can explain “new productive capacity needs corresponding economic relationships” without opening a long paragraph; the worker remains part of the productive forces; claims and proposed responses remain distinguishable; every statistic has a dated source; no invented documentary photograph or causal statistic.

## Implemented and checked

Entry now identifies the red thread as productive capacity and the surrounding structure as relations. The contemporary argument is explicit in the hero. Four recognizable digital objects and anchored labels keep people visible alongside data, AI and infrastructure. The Lab asks whether relations enable or constrain development; its two directions of mismatch remain possible. The conclusion connects adjustment with creativity and shared benefits.

Vietnam now has three source-linked cases and three relation questions for each case. The factory is an original procedural illustration, with working arm joints, a conveyor and an operator; it is not a reconstruction of the cited plant. The digital-economy field shows 100 value units, 13 red plus 0.17 of the next unit. These units represent the share of value added, not jobs, productivity or a count of firms. The map has a keyboard-accessible mobile enlargement; Hoàng Sa and Trường Sa remain explicitly labeled.

The first mobile pass put the place label against the statistic and clipped the enlarged Hoàng Sa label. Both were rejected, corrected and recaptured. The first digital pass placed labels away from their objects; labels now use their objects' projected positions. The first factory pass resembled a block toy; bevels, wheels, rollers, joints and the operator were rebuilt.

Main explanatory copy uses a short headline, one observation and one selected response. Source detail, theory qualifications and limits remain in the drawer. Interface, map and source labels add visible words beyond the V2 forty-word canvas target; this is a deliberate tradeoff to retain the user's requested geographic and factual evidence, not a claim of literal compliance with that count.
